# 🌍 ReliefMap

### Disaster Relief Coordination Platform

> **Connecting Citizens, NGOs, and Volunteers to coordinate disaster relief faster and more effectively.**

## 🚀 Live Demo

**[🌐 Open ReliefMap](https://reliefmapv1.netlify.app/)**

ReliefMap is a disaster-relief coordination platform designed to connect people who need help with organizations and volunteers who can provide it.

---

## 🎯 Problem

During disasters, people may struggle to find food, shelter, medical assistance, or other emergency support.

At the same time, NGOs may have resources, volunteers may be available, and responders may have limited visibility of where help is actually needed.

ReliefMap brings these stakeholders together through a centralized platform and interactive map to improve disaster-response coordination.

---

## 💡 Solution

**Citizen → NGO → Volunteer → Relief**

ReliefMap provides role-based workflows for:

* 👤 **Citizens** — report needs and request assistance
* 🏢 **NGOs** — coordinate requests and resources
* 🧑‍🚒 **Volunteers** — receive and complete relief missions
* 🗺️ **Responders** — visualize disaster and resource information geographically

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🔐 Citizen / NGO / Volunteer role-based workflows
